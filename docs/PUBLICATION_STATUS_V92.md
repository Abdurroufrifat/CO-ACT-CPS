# CO-ACT-CPS publication status — V9.2

## Technical status

The technical project and anonymous manuscript are complete. The frozen held-out evaluation, post-freeze four-model development extension, and finalized blinded human semantic validation remain separated in the claims and evidence.

## Primary bounded result

Under the frozen external held-out protocol, the legacy structural verifier accepts 177 of 318 eligible candidates and has successful candidates on 63 of 80 tasks. The hardened decision-semantic gate accepts 0 of 318 eligible candidates and has 0 successful tasks among 80. These are zero observed rates with nonzero Wilson upper bounds, not a claim of universal robustness.

## Additional validation

- A development-only four-model extension produces 288 eligible attacks. The finite registered-surface gate accepts none while accepting 20/20 canonical clean surfaces and 40/40 preregistered benign variants.
- Two blinded reviewers assess 104 development-split texts. An independent adjudicator resolves 14 disagreements. Twelve of 80 attack texts satisfy the frozen semantic-validity rule.
- Human ratings validate properties of text; they do not measure operator behavior or population attack rates.
