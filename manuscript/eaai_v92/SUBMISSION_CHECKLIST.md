# Author checklist before EAAI submission

The technical manuscript, figures, tables, references, and reproducibility files are complete. The authors must supply or confirm the following administrative information before submission.

- [ ] Create the separate identifying title page required for double-anonymous review: full author names, affiliations, corresponding-author email, and ORCID identifiers.
- [ ] Obtain and insert the applicable institutional ethics approval, exemption, or formal determination for the blinded text-review activity. Do not infer ethics status from the technical files.
- [ ] Add the final funding acknowledgement and grant identifiers.
- [ ] Add the final CRediT author-contribution statement.
- [ ] Confirm the competing-interest declaration.
- [ ] Confirm the generative-AI disclosure against the journal's current policy.
- [ ] Upload `highlights.txt` as the separate Highlights submission item.
- [ ] Upload only the public evidence archive as supplementary material. Never upload the private audit archive.
- [ ] Recheck every author-supplied change for accidental identity disclosure in the anonymous manuscript.
- [ ] Review the generated PDF in the submission system after source conversion.
